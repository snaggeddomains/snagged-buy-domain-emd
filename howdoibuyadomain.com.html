How Do I Buy a Domain?

There are two ways to buy a domain. If the name is available, register it at a registrar (Dynadot, Namecheap, GoDaddy) for about $10–$20 a year — buy it in two minutes. If the name is already owned by someone else, you have to find the owner, make an offer, negotiate a price, and close through escrow — or hire a buy-side broker to do it for you anonymously so the price stays fair.

Step 1: Is the domain available or already taken?

Search the exact domain at any registrar.

  • Available → add to cart, register it, done. Costs ~$10–$20/yr for a standard .com.
  • Taken (resolves to a site, a parked page, or "this domain is for sale") → you'll need to acquire it from the current owner. Keep reading.

How to buy an available domain (2 minutes)

  1. Search the name at a registrar.
  2. Add it to your cart and check out (~$10–$20/yr).
  3. Turn on auto-renew and WHOIS privacy.
  4. Point it at your website or email.

How to buy a domain that's already taken

  1. Find the owner — WHOIS lookup; use reverse-WHOIS or a broker if it's privacy-protected.
  2. Get it appraised so you know a fair number before you offer.
  3. Make an offer — anonymously. Revealing a motivated company raises the price.
  4. Negotiate to a number you both accept.
  5. Close with escrow (Escrow.com) — payment releases only after the domain transfers.
  6. Transfer the domain to your registrar and lock it.

When should I use a domain broker?

Use a broker when the name is already owned and it matters to your business — especially if the owner is hidden, unresponsive, or likely to raise the price once they know a company wants it. Frequently asked questions

Where do I buy a domain?
For an available name, any registrar (Dynadot, Namecheap, GoDaddy). For a taken name, from the current owner — directly or through a broker.
How much is a domain?
About $10–$20/yr if it's available; anywhere from hundreds to millions if someone already owns it, depending on the name.
Can I buy a domain that's taken?
Yes — nearly every domain is for sale at some price. You acquire it from the owner, ideally through escrow.
Do I own a domain forever?
You lease it — renew annually (or prepay years) to keep it. Let it lapse and it can be registered by someone else.
