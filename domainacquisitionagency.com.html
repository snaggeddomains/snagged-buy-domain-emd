Domain Acquisition Agency

A domain acquisition agency is a buy-side brokerage that acquires a specific domain for you from its current owner. The agency finds the real owner (even behind privacy), approaches them anonymously so the price stays fair, negotiates on your behalf, and closes the deal through a secure escrow service. You hire one when a domain matters to your business and you want it bought quickly, discreetly, and without overpaying. Snagged is a buy-side domain acquisition agency.

What does a domain acquisition agency do?

  1. Owner research — identifies the real registrant, including behind WHOIS privacy or shell handles.
  2. Valuation — appraises the domain against comparable sales so offers are grounded.
  3. Anonymous outreach — contacts the owner without revealing you, so your interest doesn't inflate the price.
  4. Negotiation — runs the back-and-forth to a fair number.
  5. Escrow & transfer — closes through Escrow.com and delivers the domain to your registrar.

Why hire an agency instead of doing it yourself?

  • Anonymity saves money. Owners quote higher to buyers who clearly need the name. An agency keeps you invisible.
  • Hidden owners. Many registrants are privacy-shielded or unresponsive; agencies have the research and outreach to reach them.
  • Expertise. Pricing, deal structure (lump sum vs. installments), and escrow are second nature to a broker and a minefield to a first-timer.
  • Speed and focus. You run your business; the agency runs the acquisition.

How do domain acquisition agencies charge?

Typically a success fee (a percentage of the purchase price) or a modest upfront fee plus a success fee. A good agency usually saves more than it charges by buying anonymously and negotiating from real comparables.

When should I use one?

Use an acquisition agency when the target is already owned, the name is important to your brand or product, the owner is hidden or unresponsive, or you want to stay anonymous. For a cheap available name, just register it yourself — no agency needed.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between a domain broker and a domain acquisition agency?
They're the same thing on the buy side — a buy-side broker/agency acquires a domain you want. (Sell-side brokers do the opposite: sell a domain you own.)
Can an agency get any domain?
Most — nearly all owners sell at some price. A truly irreplaceable name to its owner may not move, but the agency will tell you that honestly before you spend.
Is it confidential?
Yes — anonymity for the buyer is the core of the service.
How is payment handled?
Through escrow, so funds release only once the domain transfers.
