# Domain Acquisition Agency — Buy-Side Domain Brokers | Snagged

> A domain acquisition agency (buy-side broker) acquires a specific domain from its current owner — anonymously, at a fair price, closed through escrow.

## Key facts
- Buy-side success fee: Typically 10–20% of the purchase price
- Typical acquisition timeline: ~2–6 weeks
- Escrow (Escrow.com): Small % fee; funds released only after transfer
- Owner research: Identifies the registrant even behind WHOIS privacy
- Confidentiality: Buyer stays anonymous throughout

## Buying a domain, in short
- If the domain is available, register it at any registrar for about $10–$20/year.
- If it is already owned, approach the owner anonymously, make an appraised offer, negotiate, and close through an escrow service (Escrow.com).
- A buy-side broker does this for you: finds the owner (even behind privacy), stays anonymous so the price stays fair, negotiates, and closes through escrow.
