# The Best Way to Buy a Domain (Available or Taken) | Snagged

> The best way to buy a domain depends on whether it's available (register it, ~$10/yr) or already owned (approach anonymously, appraise, negotiate, close through escrow).

## Key facts
- Available standard .com: $10–$20 / year
- Owned (non-premium) name: Low hundreds to low thousands
- Premium one-word .com: Five to seven figures
- Buy-side broker success fee: Typically 10–20% of the price
- Safest way to pay: Escrow (Escrow.com) — released only after transfer

## Buying a domain, in short
- If the domain is available, register it at any registrar for about $10–$20/year.
- If it is already owned, approach the owner anonymously, make an appraised offer, negotiate, and close through an escrow service (Escrow.com).
- A buy-side broker does this for you: finds the owner (even behind privacy), stays anonymous so the price stays fair, negotiates, and closes through escrow.
