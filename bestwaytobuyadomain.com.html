The Best Way to Buy a Domain

The best way to buy a domain depends on one thing: is it available or already owned? If it's available, register it directly at a registrar for ~$10/yr — there's no better way. If it's already owned, the best way is to approach the owner anonymously, make an appraised offer, negotiate, and close through escrow — or hire a buy-side broker to do exactly that, because staying anonymous keeps the price fair and escrow keeps your money safe.

The best way for an available domain

Register it yourself at a reputable registrar (Dynadot, Namecheap, Porkbun, GoDaddy). Turn on auto-renew and privacy. That's the whole job — don't overcomplicate it or pay a middleman for an available name.

The best way for a domain someone already owns

  1. Don't reveal yourself. This is the single most important rule. An owner prices to the buyer — a known, motivated company pays more. Anonymous buyers pay less.
  2. Appraise before you offer. Anchor to comparable sales so you neither overpay nor insult the owner into silence.
  3. Open with a credible number, then negotiate. Leave room, move in sensible steps, hide urgency.
  4. Always close with escrow. Escrow.com holds the funds until the domain transfers — the only safe way to pay a stranger.
  5. Consider a buy-side broker for any name that matters: they keep you anonymous, reach hidden owners, negotiate, and run escrow — usually saving more than their fee.

What's the safest way to pay for a domain?

Escrow, every time. The buyer funds a licensed escrow service (Escrow.com is the standard), the seller transfers the domain, and escrow releases payment only after the transfer is confirmed. Never wire money directly to an unknown seller — direct payment is where domain scams happen.

Frequently asked questions

What's the cheapest way to buy a domain?
Register an available name yourself (~$10/yr). For an owned name, buying anonymously (so the price stays fair) is what actually keeps the cost down.
Is it safe to buy a domain from a private seller?
Only through escrow. Escrow.com protects both sides.
Should I use a broker to buy a domain?
For an available name, no. For an important name someone else owns, yes — a broker's anonymity and negotiation usually pay for themselves.
What's the best registrar to buy a domain?
Any reputable one — Dynadot, Namecheap, Porkbun, and GoDaddy are all fine for registering an available name.
